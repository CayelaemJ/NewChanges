# The Fixer Brand Release Checklist

Source of truth: **The Fixer Logo Guidelines for Partners, Version 1.0 · October 2026**.

This checklist is intentionally limited to rules stated in the supplied guideline. It does not invent typography, chart, spacing or product-design rules that the guideline does not define.

## 1. Logo artwork
- [ ] Use supplied artwork only. Do not retype, redraw, trace or rebuild the logo.
- [ ] Preserve the wordmark as one piece of artwork.
- [ ] Preserve Fixer Green exactly; never remove, recolour or replace the green bar or full stop.
- [ ] Do not crop, split or drop “The”.
- [ ] Do not stretch, squash, rotate or tilt.
- [ ] Do not add shadows, glows or outlines.

## 2. Approved logo version by background
- [ ] White / very light / Lilac background → full-colour Indigo + Green logo.
- [ ] Fixer Indigo / black / other dark calm background → approved reversed White + Green logo.
- [ ] Fixer Green → place the full-colour logo on a white panel; never directly on green.
- [ ] Mid-tone or partner-colour background → use a white panel with full clear space.
- [ ] Busy image/pattern → use a white panel.
- [ ] Light calm image area → full-colour logo may be used.
- [ ] Dark calm image area → reversed logo may be used.
- [ ] Do not use the full-colour logo directly on dark backgrounds.
- [ ] Do not use the reversed logo on light backgrounds.

## 3. Clear space and digital size
- [ ] Minimum digital logo width: **120 px**.
- [ ] Prefer **240 px artwork** for high-density screens.
- [ ] Maintain at least **2x** clear space on every side, where x is the green full-stop diameter.
- [ ] Keep the mark at least 2x from screen/page edges.
- [ ] If the space cannot support the minimum size and clear space, redesign the placement rather than shrinking the logo.

## 4. Default The Fixer product shell
- [ ] Fixer Indigo: `#2B1D73`.
- [ ] Fixer Green: `#0FC79B`.
- [ ] White: `#FFFFFF` and used generously as the main light-mode ground.
- [ ] Lilac: `#F1EFF9` as a quiet supporting panel colour.
- [ ] Indigo is structural/anchoring.
- [ ] Green is reserved primarily for emphasis, actions and progress.
- [ ] Light mode uses the full-colour logo.
- [ ] Dark mode uses the approved reversed logo.

## 5. Partner-led portal
- [ ] Partner brand leads and is the only visible brand identity inside the custom partner UI.
- [ ] Do not render “Powered by The Fixer” or a secondary The Fixer logo inside custom partner portals.
- [ ] The Fixer remains the default identity for Admin/Super Admin and the non-partner product shell.
- [ ] Partner logo keeps its own colours, clear space and brand rules.
- [ ] Admin/Super Admin control-plane shell remains The Fixer while employer content may preview/use the selected partner theme.

> Product decision: custom partner portals intentionally omit The Fixer attribution even though the supplied partner guideline describes co-branding arrangements.

## 6. Joint co-branding
- [ ] Logos appear side-by-side.
- [ ] Separate them with a thin Fixer Indigo rule.
- [ ] Keep at least 2x space on either side of the rule.
- [ ] Match visual height so neither brand dominates.
- [ ] The owner of the communication appears first.

## 7. Partner endorsement
- [ ] Endorsement mark is secondary and never larger than The Fixer.
- [ ] Do not combine the logos into one artwork/lock-up.
- [ ] Place endorsement opposite The Fixer (e.g. left/right or top/bottom).

## 8. Final pre-release checks
- [ ] Verify light mode screenshots.
- [ ] Verify dark mode screenshots.
- [ ] Verify default The Fixer user.
- [ ] Verify Admin/Super Admin.
- [ ] Verify a genuine partner login with a custom logo.
- [ ] Verify partner employer preview from Admin/Super Admin.
- [ ] Verify desktop and mobile minimum size/clear space.
- [ ] Verify PDFs/exports do not recolour or distort either logo.
- [ ] Verify browser cache versions point to the current approved assets.
- [ ] Verify automated regression checks pass.

## 9. Approval
The guideline requires approval before any item carrying The Fixer logo is printed, published or shared.

The supplied guideline leaves the brand contact and turnaround fields as placeholders. Do **not** invent those values. Add the confirmed brand contact and approval SLA here when supplied.
